Your NEAR stays staked with your validator

Each loan opens a vault account of its own. The vault stakes your NEAR with any validator on the lockup whitelist and hands the account back when you repay.

  • One vault per loan, with no upgrade method
  • Native stakers unstake once, about 28 hours
  • Rewards keep accruing in your vault
