Your NEAR stays staked with your validator

Each loan opens a vault account of its own. The vault stakes your NEAR with any validator on the lockup whitelist and hands the account back when you repay.

  • Native stakers unstake once, about 28 hours

Questions before you borrow

What if NEAR falls during a loan term?

Nothing happens until renewal. The price is read only when a term starts or renews.

Does the loan vault keep my validator?

Yes. Your loan's vault stakes with any validator on the lockup whitelist, yours included.

Where does the NEAR price come from?

Pyth Pro prices via Rhea's aggregate oracle. On testnet a relay run by the team copies it into the pool.

What can a lender lose in Ankora?

First-loss can lose everything in a crash. Fixed loses once First-loss is gone.

Has the Ankora contract been audited?

No. This is a NEAR testnet build, and the pool contract can still be upgraded by its deployer.
