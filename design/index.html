A · Due Date

Strongest fit for the mechanism: due date, renewal, notice, grace and late fee map one to one onto a library loan.

Type

Nothing to do until 10 Dec.

10 DEC 2026

Schibsted Grotesk for UI; Martian Mono, condensed, for dates and amounts.

Signature move

The next price check as a stamped date with one cell per day of the term. Past events solid, future outlined, overdue vermilion.

Motion

A new date lands like a stamp: scale 1.06 → 1 with a 2 px blur, 220 ms ease-out. Screens fade with a 6 px rise.

Honest risk

Stamps and slips can read nostalgic. It stays 2030 only if it stays crisp: no ink texture, no rotation, no paper grain.

B · Line

My pick: the only direction where the signature move is the USP itself. Its risk is that dark crypto screens are the category default.

Type

25 Nov price check

15

Archivo variable: width 100 for UI, 82–88 for station names, 70 for the big numbers.

Signature move

The term drawn as a line in its band colour: borrowed, you-are-here, price-check station, renews. A dashed red branch shows the notice path and lights only if a check fails.

Motion

The line draws on once when a loan starts (650 ms). The you-are-here ring breathes three times, then stops. Screens slide 12 px in the direction of travel.

Honest risk

Dark with bright colour is close to every DeFi app. It holds only while colours stay flat (no glow) and the map does the work.

C · Next

Strongest fit for the ADHD rule: the next step is the only yellow thing on screen.

Type

Pay 250 USDC

15

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, drawn for legibility at a glance; the mono cut for numbers.

Signature move

The next-step band: full-width yellow, black pictogram square, one line, an arrow. Days to the check at gate-number size. Grey band when there's nothing to do.

Motion

The band text changes with a split-flap, 8 ms stagger, 160 ms per character. Screens cut without fades, as signs do.

Honest risk

Yellow and black is also Binance. It stays distinct only while yellow is a ground under black type and only for the next step.

What decides it

  • A if the renewal mechanics are the story you want people to understand first.
  • B if the wick argument is the story: it is drawn, not written.
  • C if one-next-action is the story: it is the most literal answer to the ADHD rule and the most accessible type.
  • Whichever you pick, the screens, flows and copy carry over unchanged; only the tokens and the signature component change.
