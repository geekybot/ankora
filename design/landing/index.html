60–68%of liquidated borrowers were caught by an intraday low

We replayed NEAR's daily prices from October 2020 to November 2026 against Rhea's liquidation line, at about 66.5% LTV. Each simulated borrower started at 30%, 40% or 50% and added nothing for a year. Of the borrowers who got liquidated, 60–68% were caught by an intraday low on a day that closed above their liquidation price.

Each of those liquidations would have come without notice. The liquidator would have taken the collateral at a discount, at a price that was gone by the close. Ankora doesn't read the price during a term, so a wick gives it nothing to act on.

Four steps and one price check per term.

Bring NEAR

The loan's vault stakes it with your validator, as long as that validator is on NEAR Foundation's lockup whitelist. Already staked? You unstake once first, about 28 hours.

Pick a plan

The app pushes a fresh Pyth price just before you borrow. A price older than 60 seconds, or one the feed is unsure of, is rejected and your NEAR goes back.

Get dollars

The first term's interest comes off the top. Your staking rewards keep building in the vault.

Renew or repay

At the end of the term the loan renews by itself if your LTV fits a plan. Repay instead, and the vault becomes yours, with the stake still staked.

Each rate comes from pricing the loan as a put option on your collateral, using a jump-diffusion model of NEAR fitted to every day since October 2020. Once a term starts, its rate doesn't change. LTV 50% or lower

The loan renews into the plan that fits. The next term's interest is added to your debt.

LTV above 50%

72 hours of notice. Repay part of the debt in USDC to bring LTV to 50% or lower, and the loan renews at the next check. If NEAR recovers in that time, it renews without a payment.

After the grace period

A 24-hour Dutch auction of the vault, starting at its full value and falling to half. Lenders are repaid and anything above your debt goes to you. You can still repay in full until a bid lands.

No late fee

If the loan renews after its end date, you pay interest for the extra days at the new plan's rate. If an auction raises less than the debt, the lenders take the loss and you owe nothing more.

Native stakers keep their validator.

About 91% of staked NEAR, roughly $1.88B, is native stake with a validator its owner picked. Staking pools can't transfer stake between accounts, so moving it means unstaking once. Rhea doesn't accept native stake. Ankora's vault re-stakes with your validator, as long as it's on NEAR Foundation's lockup whitelist.

The move costs about 28 hours without rewards, roughly 0.017% of your stake. When you repay, the vault passes to you with the stake still staked.

Lend dollars, and choose how much of the loss you take.

Deposit USDC on NEAR and it goes straight into the pool. Every loan is backed by NEAR, so pooling can't remove crash risk. The two tranches decide who takes a loss first.

Senior: fixed 6.5% a year

  • Paid before the junior earns anything. Senior money is lost only after the borrowers' equity and the whole junior tranche are gone.
  • In a 2021–22 crash test with a rate model fitted only on later prices, senior was paid in full in every start-week when collateral sold within 2 days, and in 94.3% of them when sales took 5 days at a 10% discount.
  • Withdrawals are paid from cash the pool isn't lending. v1 has no queue, so when cash is short the first to withdraw is paid first.

Junior: first loss

  • You take losses before the senior. In a bad enough crash you lose your whole deposit.
  • You keep what the pool earns after the senior's 6.5% and Ankora's fee: 18.1–21.0% a year replaying 2021–22, in-sample; 11.3% when fitted only on 2023–26 prices, with losses in 4.8% of weeks.
  • Locked for 36 days from your latest junior deposit. After that, you can't withdraw below 15% junior cover.

How you can lose money here.

Borrowers

  • If your LTV is above 50% at a renewal and you don't pay down within 72 hours plus the 48-hour grace period, your vault is auctioned. The price can fall to half the vault's value before someone bids.
  • In our 2021–22 replay, 53–81% of borrowers who added nothing were asked to pay down at least once in a year; on Rhea, 55–85% were liquidated.
  • Repaying early doesn't refund the current term's interest.

Lenders

  • Senior and junior can both lose money in a fast NEAR crash, and the junior can lose all of it.
  • Every loan is backed by the same asset, so they all fall together. Since 2020 NEAR has fallen 52% in 3 days and 70% in 35 days.
  • If an auction gets no bid, the team sells the NEAR by hand and books the USDC back to lenders.

Everyone

  • Ankora is new code and hasn't been audited. In v1 the pool contract can still be upgraded by its deployer. The loan vaults can't be upgraded.
  • Prices come from Pyth. If the feed is stale or unsure, loans don't start.
  • We haven't tested whether enough lenders, junior buyers or auction bidders will show up.

Questions.

Is Ankora the same as Spectra Finance?

No. Ankora, spelled with a k, lends dollars against staked NEAR on the NEAR blockchain. It has no connection to Spectra Finance.

Do I have to unstake?

If your NEAR is natively staked, yes, once. It takes about 28 hours and has ranged from 21 to 35. Then you bring plain NEAR and the vault stakes it with your validator. Getting your vault back after you repay needs no unstaking.

Does Ankora use an oracle?

Yes. It reads Pyth when a loan starts and when it renews, at no other time, and uses the lower of spot and the average price. A bad price can stop a loan from starting or renewing. It can't take your collateral: only an auction can, and only after the term ends and the notice and grace periods have passed.

Can I lose my NEAR?

Yes, if your LTV is above 50% at a renewal and you don't bring it down within 72 hours plus the 48-hour grace period. The vault then goes to a 24-hour auction whose price starts at the vault's value and falls to half. Anything above your debt comes back to you. You can never owe more than the vault is worth.

What does it cost?

A fixed rate for each term. You pay the first term's interest upfront, and each later term's interest is added to the debt. There's no late fee. Ankora keeps 5% of the interest. Each vault also holds back 1.5 NEAR from what you send, and about 1.2 NEAR of that pays for the vault account's storage.

Can I repay early?

Yes, at any time, even during an auction until a bid lands. The current term's interest isn't refunded.
